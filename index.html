mmichos

A native iPhone app for your self-hosted Immich server.

Getting started

mmichos needs an Immich server that you or someone you trust runs (version 3.3 or later). Enter the server's address, then sign in with your Immich account.

Support

For questions, problems or feature requests, email support@aydenious.org.

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, mmichos version (Account → About) and Immich server version.
