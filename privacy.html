Privacy Policy

mmichos · Effective 1 November 2026

mmichos does not collect, store or share any personal data with its developer.

Your server

Photos, videos, account details and everything else the app handles are sent only to the Immich server whose address you enter. That server's operator controls that data under their own policy.

On your device

Your sign-in token is stored in the iOS Keychain. A local cache of your library, thumbnails and backup state is kept in the app's own storage and is removed when you delete the app.

With your permission, mmichos reads your Photos library to show your on-device photos and to back up the albums you choose to your server.

No tracking

mmichos contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting services or third-party tracking.
